
# Limitations

1. Reconstruction quality depends on the quality and clarity of the input sketch.
2. Complex objects may not be reconstructed accurately from a single view.
3. Generated geometry may contain minor imperfections.
4. Generation requires substantial GPU memory.
5. Model downloading and initialization can take time.
6. The generated model may require additional refinement for professional 3D printing or engineering applications.
7. Results may vary depending on the input sketch and generation prompt.
